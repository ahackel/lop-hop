LOP HOP

Space / ↑ jump (hold to jump higher) · ↓ duck · ← → pick an animal · M sound · H high scores · C credits · F full screen (or the buttons at the top left of the game). On a phone: tap the right half of the screen to jump, hold the left half to duck.
Jump the cacti, rocks and logs, duck under the branches. Running tires: eat to keep the energy up. Every bump costs energy; with none left, the animal is knocked out. Golden food, now and then high over an obstacle, gives a super power for 8 seconds: each animal its own. At night the next animal gives chase: a second bump while it is close lets it catch you. Get away till dawn to unlock it; every new day is harder.
